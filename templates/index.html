Sign reference guide

Position your hand clearly in front of the camera with good lighting. MediaPipe tracks 21 skeletal landmarks to classify these gestures:

HELLO

Open wave

Open waving hand with palm facing forward and all five fingers spread naturally wide apart.

YES

Affirmative fist

Closed fist with thumb curled across fingers in a firm upright posture.

NO

3-finger pinch

Index and middle fingers extended forward and touching thumb tip (beak pinch); ring and pinky curled into palm.

THANK YOU

Flat hand forward

Flat open hand with fingers together, moving smoothly forward toward the viewer.

HELP

Thumbs up

Thumb extended straight up firmly with all four fingers curled tightly into a fist.

STOP

Open palm upward

Flat open hand pointing straight up with palm facing camera and all five fingers held tightly together.

A

ASL letter A

Four fingers tightly curled into palm, thumb upright resting alongside index finger knuckle.

B

ASL letter B

Four fingers held straight up touching together, thumb folded flat across palm center.

C

ASL letter C

Hand arched into an open 'C' profile with fingers curved above and thumb curved below, leaving a clear opening.

D

ASL letter D

Index finger pointing straight up, thumb touching tips of middle, ring, and pinky in a loop.
